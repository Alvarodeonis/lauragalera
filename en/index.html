About me

An engineer who hires engineers

“A high-quality service built on deep specialisation and market knowledge.”

I'm an Industrial Engineer, specialising in Industrial Organisation, from Universidad Carlos III de Madrid, and I lead Robert Walters' Engineering, Energy & Industry Division in Madrid as Associate Director.

Since 2012 I have specialised in hiring executives and specialists — from middle management to CEO and Managing Director — for searches in Spain and abroad. Together with my team, I have filled more than 817 positions.

I lead a high-performing team of 13 consultants, all engineers by training. We speak the same language as the companies and professionals we work with: we understand every role, project and market from the inside.

Events

Pioneras del Progreso

The annual Madrid event that brings together women leading companies across Spain.

Annual event · Madrid, Spain

Pioneras del Progreso (“Pioneers of Progress”) is the Robert Walters event organised and presented by Laura Galera. It brings together CEOs and senior executives from leading companies across Spain in Madrid to discuss women's leadership, talent and the future of their industries.

It was created as an open forum for executives in sectors where men have traditionally been the majority, such as Energy and Industry.

  • FormatPanel discussions with leading women executives, audience Q&A and small-group debates
  • TopicsWomen's leadership, attracting and retaining talent, AI in HR and pay transparency

FAQ

Hiring an energy and industry headhunter

We are a foreign company expanding into Spain. Can you help us hire?

Yes. It is one of the most common cases: we find the Country Manager or Managing Director for Spain and help build the first local team — engineering, projects, operations and sales.

Do you know the Spanish energy and industrial market?

Laura has worked in Madrid since 2012 hiring for renewables, utilities, Oil & Gas, T&D, engineering and industry in Spain, and leads a team of 13 engineer consultants.

How long does an executive search take?

An executive search usually takes 1 to 3 months, from the first meeting with the company to selecting the right candidate.

Where is Laura based?

At Robert Walters' Madrid office (Spain), the centre of the Spanish energy market. From Madrid she also runs searches in other countries through Robert Walters' network in 31 countries.

Which positions do you fill?

Mainly leadership positions: CEO, Managing Director, Country Manager and directors of operations, projects, plants, engineering and business development.

What kind of companies do you work with?

Renewable energy companies, utilities, engineering firms and industrial companies and factories.

Where do the candidates come from?

From a database of more than 100,000 highly qualified candidates worldwide, direct headhunting and Robert Walters' international network in 31 countries.

Why hire a headhunter specialised in energy and industry?

Because they know the market, the people and the technical challenges of the sector. My team is made up entirely of engineers, so we assess every candidate with technical judgement.
