Yes. We find the Country Manager or Managing Director for Spain and build the local team — operations, grids, engineering and sales — with more than 13 years of knowledge of the Spanish market.

What is an energy sector headhunter?

An executive search consultant specialised in finding executives and specialists for energy companies: utilities, grids, Oil & Gas, renewables and hydrogen.
