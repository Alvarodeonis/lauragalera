FAQ

Renewable energy headhunter: what companies ask

What does a renewable energy headhunter do?

They identify, assess and present the executives and specialists a renewable energy company needs, with knowledge of the technologies, the projects and the market.

Do you cover all renewable technologies?

Yes: solar PV, wind, solar thermal, hydro, biomass and green hydrogen, as well as power transmission and distribution.

How long does it take to fill an executive role in renewables?

An executive search usually takes 1 to 3 months, from the first meeting with the company to selecting the right candidate.

Can you hire executives for renewable projects outside Spain?

Yes. Laura works from Madrid, Spain, and runs international searches supported by Robert Walters' network in 31 countries.
