FAQ

Industrial headhunter in Spain: what companies ask

We are entering the Spanish industrial market. Can you hire our local team?

Yes. We find the Country Manager or Managing Director for Spain and build the local team — plant management, operations, supply chain and sales — with more than 13 years of knowledge of the Spanish market.

Do you hire plant and operations directors?

Yes. They are among the roles we fill most in industry, together with Managing Director, Industrial Director and Production Director.

Do you also cover transport and logistics?

Yes. The division Laura leads covers Energy, Engineering, Industry, Transport and Logistics.

How long does it take to fill an industrial executive role?

An executive search usually takes 1 to 3 months, from the first meeting with the company to selecting the right candidate.

Do you also hire technical profiles, not just executives?

Yes. As well as leadership roles, we hire middle managers and technical specialists.
